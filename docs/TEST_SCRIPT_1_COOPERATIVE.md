# Test Script 1 — Cooperative Haider (2-way)

**YOU** = what you say (the rep). **AGENT** = roughly what the agent should say back.
The agent's exact words will differ — check the *meaning*. Order may also differ: just answer what it asks.

---

**YOU:** Meridian provider services, this is Haider. Can I get your name and the practice?
**AGENT:** Hi Haider. I'm an automated assistant calling on behalf of Cedar Park Dental in Denver. Provider is Dr. Alan Whitmore, tax ID eight four, one five five, two zero three seven, NPI one four seven seven, five eight eight, two one three. Callback is three zero three, five five five, zero one nine two.

**YOU:** And the member?
**AGENT:** Lana Kane, date of birth July ninth, nineteen eighty-three, member ID M-D-B four zero seven one nine eight eight three. She's the subscriber.

**YOU:** I have her. Coverage is active, effective March first 2024, calendar year plan. What do you need?
**AGENT:** I'm calling about scaling and root planing. What's the coverage for D4341 and D4342?

**YOU:** Periodontics is basic. Both eighty percent after deductible, in network.
**AGENT:** What's the deductible, and has she met it this year?

**YOU:** Fifty dollars individual, and it's met.
**AGENT:** What's the annual maximum, and how much is remaining?

**YOU:** Fifteen hundred, with thirteen forty remaining.
**AGENT:** What's the frequency limitation on SRP?

**YOU:** Once per quadrant every twenty-four months.
**AGENT:** Does claim history show any SRP paid — which quadrants and what dates?

**YOU:** Let me look…
**AGENT:** Sure, take your time. *(then it should WAIT quietly)*

**YOU:** I show D4341 paid on February fourth 2025, upper right and lower right.
**AGENT:** So upper left and lower left have nothing on file?

**YOU:** Correct, nothing for those.
**AGENT:** Then upper right and lower right are eligible again February fourth, twenty twenty-seven. Is that counted date of service to date of service?

**YOU:** Yes, twenty-four months from date of service.
**AGENT:** What are the documentation requirements — perio charting, radiographs, pocket depth, pre-authorization?

**YOU:** Perio charting with pocket depths and radiographs. Pockets four millimeters or more, with bone loss. Pre-treatment estimate is recommended, not required.
**AGENT:** Does SRP ever downgrade, and to what?

**YOU:** If documentation doesn't support it, it processes as a prophy.
**AGENT:** How many quadrants can be done per date of service?

**YOU:** Two.
**AGENT:** For periodontal maintenance, D4910 — what's the coverage and frequency, and is it shared with D1110?

**YOU:** Eighty percent, twice per calendar year, shared with regular prophy.
**AGENT:** Is there a waiting period between SRP and the first D4910?

**YOU:** Ninety days.
**AGENT:** Let me read this back. Lana Kane, active, calendar year plan. D4341 and D4342 at eighty percent, fifty dollar deductible met, thirteen hundred forty remaining of a fifteen hundred maximum. One per quadrant every twenty-four months. Upper right and lower right paid February fourth twenty twenty-five, upper left and lower left nothing on file. Two quadrants per date of service. Perio charting and radiographs required, four millimeter pockets with bone loss. Pre-treatment estimate recommended. Downgrades to prophy. D4910 at eighty percent, twice per calendar year shared with prophy, ninety days after SRP. Is anything wrong?

**YOU:** One thing — the remaining is now twelve forty-seven.
**AGENT:** Thanks, correcting the remaining to twelve hundred forty-seven. Can I get a call reference number and your full name?

**YOU:** Reference 771402988. Haider Ali.
**AGENT:** That's seven seven one, four zero two, nine eight eight. And to confirm, this quote is not a guarantee of payment?

**YOU:** Correct. Benefits are subject to eligibility and plan limitations at the time of service.
**AGENT:** Thank you, Haider. Goodbye.

*(Click **Disconnect**)*

## Watch for
- Replies within ~2 seconds?
- Talks over you?
- Numbers clear?
- Asks anything twice?
- Waits quietly after "Let me look…"?
- Catches the **$1,247** correction?
